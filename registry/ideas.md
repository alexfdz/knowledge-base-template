# Ideas registry

> Last updated: 2026-10-27

Goals, ideas to try and proposals: what someone wants to achieve before anyone decides it
(`kb-framework.md` §6). An idea is neither a decision nor a task. When it is decided, the row links
its `D-####`; tasks that come from an idea cite it in their `fuente` (`idea I-####`, §9). The
interpretation (priority, how it fits) lives in [`context.md`](../context.md) and in the projects.

Type: `goal` (an outcome being pursued) · `idea` (something to try) · `proposal` (something to
take to the governing body)
Status: `open` · `in-progress` · `done` · `dropped`
