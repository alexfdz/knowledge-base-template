# People registry

> Last updated: 2026-08-27

Members, contacts, collaborators, stakeholders and partners. In most subjects people are central,
so they are a first-class entity (`kb-framework.md` §10). Stable facts only; campaign state lives
in [`../people/trackers/`](../people/trackers/), accumulated context in `people/<slug>.md`.

> **Private data.** If this registry holds contact details, keep the repo private
> (`kb-framework.md` §13). Record names, roles and work contact only — **not** national ID
> numbers or home addresses; those stay in the source documents.

Roles (starter set — redefine for your subject): `lead` · `contributor` · `stakeholder` ·
`contact` · `partner`
Status: `active` · `prospect` · `lapsed` · `alumnus`
